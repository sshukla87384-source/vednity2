Last updated: September 14, 2026

Vednity ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through www.vednity.com, how we use it, and your rights.

Information you provide: when you submit our contact or newsletter form we collect your name, email address, phone number, company details and the message you send.

Information collected automatically: like most websites we may collect IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited and referring URLs via cookies and analytics tools (e.g. Google Analytics).

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiries and provide our services

3. Cookies

We use essential cookies to make the site work and, with your consent, analytics and advertising cookies (e.g. Google Analytics, Meta Pixel) to understand traffic and measure campaigns. You can manage or withdraw consent through the cookie banner or your browser settings.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it with trusted service providers (hosting, email, analytics, CRM) who process it on our behalf under confidentiality obligations, or when required by law.

5. Data retention

We keep enquiry data for as long as needed to serve you and for a reasonable period afterwards for record-keeping, unless you ask us to delete it.

6. Your rights

Depending on your location (including under GDPR, the DPDP Act 2023 and CCPA) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, object to processing, or withdraw consent. To exercise these rights email hello@vednity.com.

7. Security

We use HTTPS encryption and industry-standard safeguards. No method of transmission is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Third-party links

Our site may link to external websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

9. Children

Our services are not directed to children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data.
